LEVEL 02

Networking

Learn how devices communicate. Networking knowledge is essential for understanding both attacks and defense.

02.01
🌐

IP Addresses

02.02
🔌

Ports & Protocols

Firewalls & VPNs

IMPORTANT

Practice with permission.

Cybersecurity skills should be developed using systems you own, authorized environments, educational laboratories and legal CTF platforms. Never test or attack a real system without explicit permission.
