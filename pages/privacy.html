PRIVACY

YOUR DATA
MATTERS.

This Privacy Policy explains how The Kingdom Before Sunrise approaches information and privacy on this website.

01 / INTRODUCTION

Privacy Comes First.

The Kingdom Before Sunrise is designed primarily as an educational and informational cybersecurity platform.

This Privacy Policy describes the general approach to information handled through the website in its current form.

The website may change over time. If new features are introduced that affect privacy or data processing, this policy may be updated accordingly.

02 / CURRENT WEBSITE

How The Site Currently Works

01

Static Content

The current website is primarily composed of static educational pages and resources.

02

No User Accounts

The current version does not provide user registration or personal accounts.

03

No Password System

The current website does not ask users to create passwords or authentication credentials.

04

No Payment System

The current website does not directly process payments or financial transactions.

03 / INFORMATION

Information We May Receive

The current static website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information simply because someone visits a page.

However, information may potentially be processed by the hosting provider or other technical infrastructure used to operate the website.

Depending on the hosting environment, technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information, timestamps or server logs may be processed by the infrastructure provider.

Such processing may be controlled by the relevant hosting provider's own policies and technical configuration.

04 / CONTACT

Contact Information

The current Contact page contains a form interface, but the form is not connected to a backend or message-storage system in the current version.

Therefore, the current form should not be treated as a functioning method of submitting or storing personal information.

If a future version connects the form to an email provider, database, form-processing service or other system, the privacy information on this page should be updated before that feature is launched.

05 / SECURITY

Do Not Submit Sensitive Information.

01

Passwords

Never submit account passwords through educational forms or messages.

02

Private Keys

Never publish private cryptographic keys, recovery keys or secret credentials.

03

Financial Information

Do not submit banking information, payment credentials or similar financial data.

04

Personal Secrets

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for the purpose of contacting the platform.

06 / COOKIES

Cookies And Local Storage

The current version of the website is not designed to intentionally use tracking cookies for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Future features may use cookies, local storage or similar browser technologies where necessary for functionality.

If such technologies are introduced, the website's privacy information should be updated to explain what they do and why they are used.

07 / ANALYTICS

Analytics And Tracking

The current version does not claim to use a third-party analytics service.

If analytics, advertising, performance monitoring or other tracking technologies are added later, their use should be reviewed and this policy should be updated before implementation.

08 / THIRD PARTIES

External Websites And Services

The platform may contain links to external websites, documentation, courses, laboratories, tools, organizations and other resources.

Once you leave The Kingdom Before Sunrise and visit another service, that service's own privacy policy, terms and practices may apply.

Users should review the privacy information of external services before providing them with personal information.

09 / DATA SECURITY

Protecting Information

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be considered when handling personal information associated with the website.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security against every possible threat.

Website operators, hosting providers and third-party services may each have different security responsibilities depending on how the platform is implemented.

10 / YOUNG USERS

Students And Young Learners

The platform is intended to provide educational information that may be useful to students and learners.

The website should not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from young users.

Where applicable, users and their parents or guardians should consider the privacy requirements that apply in their jurisdiction and to the services being used.

11 / RETENTION

Data Retention

Because the current website does not operate a user-account database or connected contact-message storage system, it does not currently define a separate platform retention period for user accounts or submitted messages.

Technical records maintained by hosting or infrastructure providers may be subject to their own retention policies.

12 / UPDATES

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website gains new features, services or methods of handling information.

The most recent version published on this website should be considered the current version.

Significant changes should be reflected in the policy before or when the relevant feature becomes active.

13 / IMPORTANT

Privacy Laws Can Differ.

Privacy and data-protection requirements can vary depending on the user's location, the website operator's location, the hosting environment and the services connected to the website.

This page provides general information about the intended privacy approach of the platform and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.
