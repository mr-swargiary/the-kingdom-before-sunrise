SECURITY

Responsible Security Reporting

Security researchers and users who discover a potential vulnerability should avoid exploiting, altering or accessing information beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue safely.

01

Minimize Impact

Avoid actions that could damage systems, interrupt services or expose private information.

02

Protect Evidence

Record enough information to explain the issue without collecting unnecessary sensitive data.

03

Report Clearly

Explain what happened, where it occurred and how the issue can be reproduced safely.

04

Respect Authorization

Only test systems where you have permission or where testing is explicitly authorized.
